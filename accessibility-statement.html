Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 2026

Immuna is built for people managing chronic and autoimmune conditions, many of whom rely on assistive technology or need flexibility in how they interact with the web. Making this site and the eventual app accessible isn't an afterthought for us — it matters to who we're building for.

Our aim

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. This is an ongoing goal rather than a certification — we're a small, pre-launch site and are actively working to identify and fix gaps.

What we've done so far

  • Used semantic HTML structure for headings and content
  • Maintained colour contrast between text and backgrounds where possible
  • Made text resizable without breaking layout
  • Kept the layout responsive across screen sizes

Known limitations

As a new site, there are likely areas that haven't been fully tested with screen readers or other assistive technology yet. We're aware this is an active area for improvement, particularly as we move from this waitlist page to the full app.

Feedback

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this site, please let us know. We take accessibility feedback seriously and will do our best to address issues promptly.
