Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Immuna collects through this website, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

Who we are

Immuna is building an app to help people with autoimmune conditions manage medications, appointments, symptoms, and connect with others. This website is currently a pre-launch waitlist page.

What we collect

If you join our waitlist, we collect the email address you provide. We don't ask for or collect any other personal or health information through this website.

Why we collect it

  • To let you know when Immuna launches
  • To occasionally share meaningful updates about our progress

We will not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies. If you accept all cookies via the banner, we may use additional cookies to understand how visitors use the site. If you choose "Essential only," we only use cookies strictly necessary for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie preference itself).

How long we keep it

We'll keep your email address on our waitlist until Immuna launches and you've either signed up for the app or asked us to remove it, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw consent and be removed from the waitlist at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at eloisebuckingham@immuna.co.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Immuna develops, particularly once the app itself launches and handles health-related data. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to eloisebuckingham@immuna.co.uk.
